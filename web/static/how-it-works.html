How it works

This page restates what the code and atlas setup docs say about each stage. It does not add clinical claims.

Pipeline

  1. Scans in

    BraTS-style cases supply four MRI volumes (FLAIR, T1, T1 with contrast, T2). Real hospital studies are walked as DICOM trees; the selected FLAIR, T1, T1c, and T2 series are converted. Output folders and NIfTI headers for real studies carry a hashed study id only — no names, raw IDs, dates, or source folder names.

  2. Clean-up and alignment

    For real studies the pipeline N4-corrects, rigid-registers to a 1 mm RAS reference, skull-strips once, and writes nonzero z-scored volumes. BraTS cases used for training and scoring are already skull-stripped; the brain mask is built from any modality above its background, with closing and hole filling, keeping the largest connected component.

  3. Tumor segmentation

    A SegResNet predicts three probability heads — enhancing tumor (ET), tumor core (TC), and whole tumor (WT) — with Gaussian sliding-window inference on a 96³ crop and flip test-time averaging in probability space. Post-processing applies thresholds from the model’s YAML config, enforces ET ⊂ TC ⊂ WT, removes small components, and applies the ET size rule. Nested tumor shells used later treat edema as WT, core as TC, and enhancing as ET.

  4. Anatomy

    When anatomy is enabled, parcellate_case runs the DIPY PyTorch SynthSeg port. SynthSeg labels follow the FreeSurfer lookup that model writes. That port has no Desikan–Killiany switch: cortex is one label per hemisphere. Subcortical SynthSeg labels are kept; frontal, parietal, temporal, occipital, and insular lobes are painted from the FSL MNI structural atlas warped with the ANTs transforms cached for tracts. The result is resampled with nearest neighbours onto the tumor grid, the dilated whole-tumor mask is cleared from every label, and anatomy_seg.nii.gz is written.

  5. Fibre pathways

    BraTS has no diffusion MRI. Pathway meshes are expected locations from the JHU white-matter tractography atlas warped from MNI into the patient T1 — not patient tractography. Every tract object is stamped source="atlas-estimated". SyN registers MNI152 1 mm T1 to the patient T1; the cost-function mask is the patient brain with the whole tumor removed after a 5-voxel dilation. Tract maps are warped with linear interpolation; voxels at or above the probability threshold (default 0.25) are meshed. Required atlas files are never downloaded; missing files raise an error pointing at the atlas setup docs. Optic radiation is optional and skipped with a warning when absent.

  6. Meshing

    Each binary mask is Gaussian-smoothed, padded, converted with marching cubes, Taubin-smoothed (no Laplacian), then transformed to scanner millimetres. Optional quadric decimation can reduce face count. Scene export builds tumor compartments, then glass hemispheres and interiors; lobe ids stay on cortex vertices and in the location summary and are not separate solid meshes.

  7. Viewing

    The Flask app serves scene.glb, manifest.json, and MRI slice images. The browser viewer loads the scene with three.js, legend groups, optional guided tour, and the note that tracts are atlas-estimated, not patient tractography.
